## Overview

Add diagnostic metrics and a field selector that enhance the simulator's pedagogical value without cluttering the primary interface. These are refinements that build on the three-view foundation.

## Subtasks

### T13a: Compact Numerical Diagnostics

**New/renamed metrics**:

| Current | New | Formula | Notes |
|---------|-----|---------|-------|
| — | Courant number | `λ = c·dt/dx` (classical) or `Δτ/Δσ` (relativistic) | Stability indicator |
| "Causality" | Max transverse speed | `max_σ |∂y/∂τ|` | Rename for clarity |
| — | Energy drift | `(E(τ) − E(0)) / E(0)` | Conservation check in relativistic mode |

**Acceptance**: All metrics update in real time; energy drift stays < 1% for long runs in relativistic mode.

### T13b: Energy Strip Labeling

- Make energy density strip **optional** (toggle in settings)
- Label explicitly: `ℰ(σ, τ₀)` with formula:
  ```
  ℰ = ½μ(∂τy)² + ½τ(∂σy)²
  ```
- Use **sequential palette** for energy (nonnegative) vs diverging for displacement

---

### T13c: Worldsheet Field Selector

A dropdown or button group above the worldsheet to select the displayed field:

| Field | Symbol | Meaning | Best Use |
|-------|--------|---------|----------|
| Transverse displacement | `y` | Default | Wave propagation |
| Transverse velocity | `∂τ y` | Time derivative | Turning points, moving packets |
| Energy density | `ℰ` | Local energy | Energy flow, localization |
| Local slope | `∂σ y` | Space derivative | Steepening, mode structure |

**Implementation**: Each field is derived from the stored history buffer. For `∂τ y`, compute finite differences in time; for `∂σ y`, compute finite differences in space.

**Color mapping**:
- Signed fields (`y`, `∂τ y`, `∂σ y`): diverging palette, dark neutral at 0, cyan positive, magenta negative
- Nonnegative field (`ℰ`): sequential palette
- Fixed symmetric scale `[-y_max, y_max]` over displayed temporal window
