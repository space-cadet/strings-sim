## Description
Define and implement model-appropriate external-field effects across the classical transverse solver, the linearized relativistic reference solver, T18 constrained classical evolution, and future T19/T20 extensions. The shared interface must not imply that one force law has the same physical meaning in every model.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A typed external-field contract declares units, support, evaluation inputs, and coupling semantics.
- [ ] Classical and linearized-relativistic forcing includes work, energy injection, damping loss, and boundary-flux diagnostics.
- [ ] T18 forcing preserves or explicitly reports conformal constraints, closure, and target-space interpretation.
- [ ] T19 background driving is represented as a finite Hamiltonian coupling and preserves the free reference path.
- [ ] T20 background effects are kept separate from topology-only visualization unless a worldsheet/amplitude task is approved.

## Implementation Details
Use one registry for field identity and parameter metadata, with model-specific adapters:

- T2 classical: an additive transverse source in the damped wave equation.
- T5/T17 relativistic reference: a weak transverse source in natural units, with the free path unchanged at zero amplitude.
- T18: a target-space force density coupled through the constrained conformal-gauge formulation; do not add an unconstrained acceleration after reconstruction.
- T19: a finite Hamiltonian drive on the oscillator state, clearly labelled as no longer free.
- T20: reserve worldsheet background couplings for a separately specified amplitude/correlator task.

The first field catalogue should stay small: none, uniform, localized Gaussian, sinusoidal drive, and finite pulse. Each field must expose its units, spatial profile, time dependence, direction, amplitude, and supported models.

Energy is not expected to be conserved under forcing. Record field work, damping loss, boundary flux, and residual energy-balance error instead.
